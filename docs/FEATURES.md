# Features and functionality

## Company workspace

- Multiple independent companies with home currency, legal identity, TRN, address, contact, and bank details.
- Multiple inventories/warehouses per company, each with its own code, invoice prefix, and next invoice number.
- Company and inventory selectors that scope forms, lists, dashboard data, and most reports.
- Per-user company assignments, global administration, company administration, and six operational roles.
- Configurable login branding, company logos, background, display name, copyright text, document colors, templates, and letterhead.
- Light and dark appearance modes plus a per-user accent colour (Emerald, Ocean, Indigo, Violet, Rose or Amber), both chosen in the header Appearance menu and saved to the user's account, plus a live header panel, available to every role including Sales, showing online users with their last-activity date and time (UAE) on one line; only administrators also see sign-in IP addresses.

## Sales and customers

- Customer records with company, international phone/WhatsApp validation, country, reseller/planet fields, TRN, email, credit, and currency.
- Quotations, estimates, proforma invoices, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, statement charges, finance charges, and credit memos.
- Estimate, proforma invoice, sales order, and invoice entry forms offer Add Line below the last item row so long documents can grow without scrolling to the top. Their posting-account dropdown defaults to the selected customer’s linked Accounts Receivable account in the document currency.
- Partial conversion of estimates, proformas, and sales orders into one or more invoices with source-line allocation tracking.
- Customer payment entry against one or multiple open invoices, including partial allocations and refreshed invoice status.
- Deleting an invoice voids it: it stays in Sales with status Void, its item history and a stock-return movement are kept, its journal is unposted, and the row shows who voided it, the date and time (Dubai) and the reason. The Sales **Status** filter shows Not void (default), Void invoices, or All, including void. Void invoices cannot be edited, delivered, voided again or given new or edited packing lists (saved packing lists stay viewable and printable); their document view hides Ship To, Mark as delivered and inventory item changes and shows who voided it, when and why, and appear in the Voided/Deleted Transactions report.
- Customer center with balances, activity, open balances, statements, overdue analysis, and source document drill-down. Its **Customer workflows** panel offers quotations, estimates, proforma invoices, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, payments, credit notes and the customer reports; statement and finance charges are not in that panel (they remain available from Home).
- Customer and supplier statements can include a movable bank details box: the Company Setup bank account for the statement currency (bank, account name, number, IBAN, SWIFT, currency) and its remark, editable for that statement. It prints after the statement table at the chosen left offset in screen print, standard PDF and custom-template PDF output, with the company stamp placed after it.
- Customer and supplier statements use the letterhead assigned to **Statement** in every output. Screen print shows its header (logos, heading, contact line) and footer, prints customer and reference cells as plain text, leaves out the on-screen reporting-basis and summary panels (the statement heading carries the totals), stays light in dark mode, and prints the company stamp after the table and bank boxes at its Left offset. The A4 PDF export draws the same letterhead header and footer on every page with a "Statement of Account" summary, a "Statement for … · period" line and a light table; the stamp position guide shows that header. Without an assigned letterhead the generic report PDF layout is used.
- Payment terms and calculated due dates. **Due on receipt** is the default on every customer document: new documents, invoices converted from quotations, estimates, proformas and sales orders without terms, and older documents opened for editing with blank terms. The server saves blank customer-document terms as Due on receipt; bills and other vendor documents are unchanged.

## Purchasing and vendors

- Vendor records with contact, company, country, TRN, currency, payable balance, and change history.
- Purchase orders, item receipts, received-item bills, bills, expenses, vendor credits, and bill payments.
- Supplier bills using the `Goods imported into the UAE` VAT code require Bill of Entry and Airway Bill references, support customs-document attachments, and keep those references on the saved and printed bill. When converting an import-coded Purchase Order, the UAE import document fields are shown immediately and become required as soon as an import line is included in the Bill. Purchases includes a company- and inventory-scoped Bill of Entry No. search whose results open the original supplier bill.
- Bills and purchase orders default their posting-account dropdown to the selected vendor’s linked Accounts Payable account in the document currency.
- Purchase order and bill entry forms offer Add Line below the last item row.
- Partial purchase-order receiving with remaining quantities and receipt allocation tracking.
- Purchase returns constrained to quantities still available from source bills.
- Single or multi-bill vendor payment allocation and refreshed bill status.
- Vendor center with balances, purchasing history, returns, open payables, aging, and vendor-currency reports.
- Cargo List (sidebar, under Vendors): a shared list of cargo and freight agents for the company, with name, telephone, mobile, email, modes (Air, Sea, Land, Courier), destination countries, address and notes. Every user role can view it and add entries; the person who added an entry, or an administrator, can edit it; only administrators delete. It has search, page size and paging, a desktop table and mobile cards.
- Supplier bank details (bank name, account name, account number, IBAN, SWIFT/BIC) on each vendor, entered in the new-vendor form or Edit, shown on the Vendor Center supplier profile, and printable as a movable box on purchase orders, bills and single-vendor Vendor Statements.
- Supplier Centre Quick report and Open balance use the selected vendor’s currency, including foreign-currency vendors in a company with a different home currency.

## Inventory

- Stock, non-stock, service, other charge, subtotal, group, discount, payment, VAT item, and VAT group item types.
- Unique item numbers and SKU controls per inventory location.
- Quantity on hand, reorder points, sales prices, unit cost, GRN cost, serial numbers, and active status.
- Inventory items load independently from transactions, contacts, and account balances; historical average cost and open-PO quantities are aggregated in PostgreSQL, and long item lists are paginated for responsive browsing.
- In the Inventory list and on each Inventory Overview product line (the trailing #Item No.), resting the mouse on an Item No. for a moment copies it to the clipboard (with a "copied" notice); clicking it, or Enter/Space when focused, also copies it, for browsers such as Safari that only allow copying after a click. Clicking the #Item No. on an Inventory Overview line copies it instead of opening the product details popup; the rest of the line still opens it.
- Authorized inventory editors can mark each item inactive or active from its row. Inactive items move out of All items into the Item is inactive tab, and reactivated items return to All items. The inactive status label applies even at zero quantity; active stock counts exclude inactive items. Active items with zero (or negative) quantity on hand move out of All items into the **Out of stock** tab and return to All items once stock is received; the separate **Out of stock · Other companies** tab lists catalogue items not yet in the selected inventory.
- Item specifications with reusable option values and generated descriptions.
- **Duplicate item** copies only the item's description and specifications (with its category, type, accounts and VAT codes) into an unsaved draft with a new SKU and item number. Everything else starts empty for you to fill: opening quantity 0 (enforced by the server), cost 0 (enforced), sales price 0, reorder point 0, no preferred supplier and no open-PO quantity.
- The Items list **Avg. cost** column shows the average purchase cost from the item's own bills and item receipts (falling back to the last purchase price, then the saved Cost when it has none), the same figure used for cost of sales.
- Inventory item rows show one uppercase line ordered as brand/model, generated SKU, condition such as `BRAND NEW`, remaining specifications, and the item number as a final `#` suffix. Generated and displayed descriptions omit every standalone `NO` specification. Inventory Overview email, WhatsApp, and Telegram copies retain that complete description and Item #; social copies use the same `🔺` product-title and `⚡` condition format for Brand New, Open Box, Refurbished, and Used items while omitting inventory-location lines.
- HS code, country of origin, dimensions, and weight maintenance.
- Inventory **Import** (users who can add items): upload Excel (.xlsx, Excel XML / HTML .xls such as the app's own exports) or CSV into the selected inventory. A header row with Name / Item / Item & description / Description is required; SKU, Item No., Category, Quantity (opening quantity, same as Add new), Reorder point, Sales price, Cost, Status and Type map to item fields, and any other named column becomes a specification. Prices such as `AED 6,000.00` are accepted. The dialog previews and flags bad rows, then saves each row through `POST /api/records` like Add new (generated SKUs/item numbers, unique-name check) and lists the result per row. Up to 2,000 rows per file; old binary .xls files must be re-saved as .xlsx or CSV. .xls markup (Excel XML or HTML tables) is read as plain text, never parsed as a web page. A CSV template is downloadable (`lib/item-import.ts`, `app/item-import.tsx`).
- Product Customization Details stores RAM, Storage, **Screen Details**, Part Number, Serial, UPC No. and details for users per product. RAM, Storage and Screen Details fall back to the item's RAM/Memory, Storage/SSD and Screen/Display specifications when empty. Screen Details is an editable column on the Customization Details page, included in its search and exports, and shown in both read-only detail popups (Customization Details and Inventory Overview). When entered, it is also added to Inventory Overview WhatsApp, Telegram and email copies (a "Screen:" line under the product title) and as a Screen Details column in its Excel exports. Column `items.customization_screen`, migration 0097.
- Consolidated inventory overview, out-of-stock/reorder view, and serial purchase/sales history. The Sales role can open the complete read-only Inventory Overview for its assigned companies instead of the limited Incoming items screen. Sales users also see the New Arrival, Price Change and Just Sold tabs, without supplier or customer names, document numbers or purchase prices. Every role can tick several Inventory Overview products one by one and copy them (WhatsApp, Telegram, email) or export them to Excel; only administrators can use select-all. Every role can tick individual Inventory Overview products and copy them in WhatsApp, Telegram or email format; select-all and the Excel exports are administrator-only. The page opens with one full-width, colour-coded filter bar: All (total units), each inventory, In stock, Low stock, Out of stock, On hold, and Received 7 days / 30 days, each with its count (clicking an active chip clears it). The Consolidated stock card holds search and refresh, the Category/Brand/price filters, then a share bar (price display options on the left, copy and Excel actions on the right). Each product row shows its full description (click it to open the details popup), a stock-status pill, inventory chips, hold badge, received age and Details/Hold actions; Qty and Price are right-aligned with free/held and VAT notes. Every Inventory Overview product shows how many days ago it was last received and provides a read-only Product Customization Details popup to all roles with inventory access. The activity tabs (Incoming, New Arrival, Price Change, Just Sold) start collapsed with their counts visible; clicking a tab drops its list down, and clicking the open tab again collapses it. Incoming links every outstanding quantity to its Purchase Order, labels it ready for Bill conversion or identifies its latest partial Bill, and removes converted/fully received POs. New Arrival lists stock received through saved Enter Bill documents for 12 hours from the bill creation timestamp, with supplier, received quantity, purchase price, and a View link to the bill. Price Change keeps only each item's latest valid selling-price change for 12 hours. Time-limited entries expire while the overview is open.
- Inventory check reports with selected in-stock items, actual counts, and cross-company quantity comparison.
- Multi-line transfers between inventories or companies, with revisions and reversal.
- Stock pricing with conflict detection and stock revaluation controls.
- Shared-item comparison and copying across accessible companies.
- SKU reservations and database locks to prevent overlapping inventory edits.

## Accounting and banking

- Company chart of accounts with standard system roles, multi-currency control accounts, parent/sub-account structure, and opening balances.
- Automatic double-entry journals for posting sales, purchases, payments, banking, VAT, and stock effects.
- Manual general journal entries with balanced debit/credit validation and inventory location selection.
- Deposits, cheques, credit-card charges, transfers, cheque orders, and opening balances.
- UAE bank cheque preparation for supplier, salary, and expense workflows with bank-specific layouts and print alignment.
- Write UAE Bank Cheque is available in Banking workflows; the sidebar omits the duplicate cheque shortcut.
- Account history, general ledger, trial balance, balance sheet, cash flow, receivables, payables, and reconciliation-oriented reports.
- Audit log coverage for sensitive changes such as vendor changes and destructive actions.

## Warranty, packing, and attachments

- Warranty/RMA slips linked to customers, invoices, invoice items, suppliers, supplier bills, serials, dates, and service status. Supplier bills open a prefilled RMA draft; selecting an invoiced item automatically links its supplier: the bill or received item bill with the same serial number, otherwise the latest such bill for the item on or before the invoice date, otherwise the item's preferred supplier (no bill); a note under the item says which was used; the same link is filled automatically when a slip with an invoiced item but no supplier is opened, and on save (API) when no supplier is sent; and the RMA list and form link back to the saved bill.
- A4 warranty service records with working portrait/landscape preview, print, and PDF output. Minimizing a draft returns the user to the application while preserving every entered field in a floating Restore bar; closing that bar prompts before discarding the draft.
- Supplier return receipts use the saved RMA reference and contain the supplier, linked bill number, purchase date, serial number, problem, return date, accessories, and supplier acknowledgment. Marking a return opens the receipt preview; A4 print/PDF require the saved return details. The customer service slip remains selectable, and company letterhead/stamp settings are reused.
- Packing lists generated from invoice lines with editable cartons, references, weights, dimensions, quantities, and serial details.
- Transaction and employee attachment upload, listing, download, and deletion with company/entity access checks.

## Document output

- Classic, modern, and minimal company document templates with configurable color.
- Tax invoice, proforma invoice, commercial invoice, delivery note, packing list, and HS-code summary views.
- Company letterhead builder and named A4 letterhead documents. The editor is grouped into Letterhead (name, colour presets), Header (left/right logos, optional phone/email/website line, heading, subtitle, website), Letter content (rich text, optional text position), Company stamp, Footer and document assignment. The right logo is aligned to the right margin on the letterhead and on every document that uses its header. The footer is pinned to the bottom of the page in a thin-line or colour-band style, with optional footer text, company address, contact line and TRN. Every document type ticked under "Use this letterhead on" uses that letterhead, whichever way it prints: the standard template, a saved document design, or a Layout Builder layout (the letterhead header replaces the layout's company header, or is added when the layout has none, and its footer repeats on every printed page). Invoices, quotations, estimates, proformas, sales orders, bills, purchase orders, purchase returns, credit notes, refunds, sales receipts and delivery notes show the letterhead header and footer; packing lists and HS code summaries add its footer; A/P Aging Summary, Supplier QuickReport and Supplier Open Balance show its header. The footer also prints on documents the letterhead is assigned to: at the bottom of the page on the Warranty / RMA slip and Customer Payment Receipt, after the content on customer and vendor statements, A/P Aging Summary, Supplier QuickReport, Supplier Open Balance and the Staff Salary Sheet (on every page of its PDF). The RCM Declaration keeps its own footer, which already includes the letterhead footer text. Documents without an assigned letterhead are unchanged. Save is enabled only when there are unsaved changes.
- Company logos, bank details, VAT identity, extra fields, line comments, serial numbers, and payment terms.
- Item lines on invoices, pending invoices, bills, purchase orders, item receipts and received item bills have Comments and Serial Number boxes under the description, on create and edit (`lib/line-extras.ts`). Receiving a purchase order pre-fills each line's comments and serial numbers on the bill, still editable. Invoicing from a sales source and receiving a purchase order into a bill only have these boxes on each line (there is no separate document-level Comments / Serial Number box).
- Paid and company stamp overlays that can be moved before printing/export where supported.
- A4 portrait print/PDF output by default, with a portrait/landscape selector; packing lists default to landscape.

## Window controls

- Application dialogs use a responsive landscape workspace on desktop and provide minimize, maximize/restore, and close controls without discarding in-progress form data. Users can minimize multiple transaction, item, contact, account, RMA, and other supported windows, continue working elsewhere, and restore each independent draft from a stacked window dock without one minimized form overwriting another.
- Success, warning, information, and error notifications use solid high-contrast colors that remain readable in light and dark mode, including while a dialog is open.

## VAT code register

- VAT Codes always retain six standard UAE choices, including `Goods imported into the UAE`, and allow administrators to add, edit, deactivate, or safely delete unused custom codes. The Status column switch makes any code inactive or active again, including the standard EXEMPT, REVERSE_CHARGE, IMPORT_GOODS and OUT_OF_SCOPE codes; STANDARD and ZERO always stay active because documents apply them automatically (default and zero-rate lines). Inactive codes disappear from document and item dropdowns until reactivated. Each code shows its linked documents and item defaults, supports evidence attachments, and exports as an A4 portrait print/PDF register with an optional movable company stamp.
- Customer Payments are non-VAT receipts: the entry screen hides VAT Code and VAT Amount, saves a payment method and separate reference number, links supporting attachments, and opens a fitted A4 portrait print/PDF receipt with an optional movable company stamp.

## Reporting

Vendors reports use a deduplicated ten-report library grouped into balances/aging, supplier activity and trends. Views provide search, supplier, posted account, status, document-type and past-due filters, matching summaries, desktop numeric tables and tablet/mobile cards. A4 print/PDF, CSV and Excel follow the displayed rows; Excel includes a Vendors summary sheet and identifies transaction currency. Posted payable or settlement accounts link to account history with existing permissions; planned purchase orders and missing or ambiguous historical links remain labelled. Running balances and unit prices are never summed. Supplier balance summaries and detail retain all prior activity through the selected as-of date. Posted payable entries classify charges and settlements; cash-paid expenses do not increase payable balances, and bill-linked bank cheques reduce balances and statements. Statements keep their server-side supplier, currency and date filters and opening/closing totals. Vendor stamps use the scaled A4 mouse/touch, keyboard and millimetre preview.

Sales reports use a deduplicated library grouped by daily activity, customers/delivery, products, performance/trends and pipeline/fulfilment. Search, customer/rep/account/status filters and numeric sorting drive the same summaries, charts, table/cards and A4 print/PDF, CSV and Excel downloads. Desktop aligns quantities and prices; tablet/mobile retain labelled details. Detail links open actual posted receivable/receipt and revenue accounts, while unposted documents retain saved planned accounts. Daily summaries count unique documents and use document totals; freight-only lines are excluded from unit counts. Sales Order Fulfilment shows ordered, invoiced and remaining quantities from current allocations. Excel includes a Sales summary sheet. The Sales stamp control provides a proportional A4 preview, mouse/touch dragging, keyboard movement and millimetre offsets shared by print/PDF.

Purchases reports are grouped by supplier spending, purchases by sales rep, item purchasing and orders/receiving. Purchases by Sales Rep (summary and detail) groups bills, received-item bills, purchase expenses and vendor credits (negative) by the Sales Rep saved on each document, in home currency, excluding cancelled/void documents; detail rows open the source document and its posted payable/payment account. Views include search, supplier/sales-rep/account/status filters, numeric sorting, matching summaries and report totals, desktop tables and tablet/mobile cards. A4 print/PDF, CSV and Excel use the displayed rows; Excel includes a Purchases summary sheet. Supplier spend excludes VAT consistently with detail. Outstanding purchase orders subtract saved receiving allocations, show commitments before VAT in home currency, and separate closed/cancelled orders in status counts. Detail account links use actual posted journal credits for bills/expenses; purchase orders show their saved planned account because they do not post. Missing or ambiguous historical account links are shown for review.

VAT reports use a grouped, deduplicated seven-report library with company-wide scope, code/rate labels, text/code/direction/account/status filters, filtered summaries and responsive table/cards. Detail VAT is signed for credit notes and includes per-line freight in taxable values; item groups separate saved codes/rates and input/output direction. Documents open their original source, VAT codes open a filtered register, and verified VAT accounts open ledger history. The code register shows current system VAT controls, not code-specific overrides. VAT Position Summary shows document output/input/net before manual adjustments rather than a complete VAT201 return. A4 portrait/landscape print/PDF, CSV and Excel share the visible rows and additive totals; unit prices and rates are never summed. VAT uses the proportional movable stamp preview.

The Layout Builder line-items table prints each line's comments and serial numbers (as `S/N: …`, side by side and comma-separated, wrapping across the column, whether typed one per line or with commas) under its description by default (**Comments and serial numbers under the description**, on for older layouts too); a field that has its own Comments or Serial column stays in that column only. Customer message, terms and text sections keep English and Arabic together: each line takes its own direction (Arabic lines right-to-left and right-aligned), `**text**` prints bold and lines starting with `#` print as headings, with the marks hidden. Their **Arabic and English side by side** switch puts lines containing Arabic in a right-hand right-to-left column and the other lines in a left-hand column, each in original order. Every Layout Builder section has a **Shrink text to fit the row** style switch: its text gets smaller (down to 40% of its size) until the section is no taller than the other sections in its row, for example warranty terms beside the totals ending level with Paid. It is applied on the editor canvas, previews and the print/PDF frame (`lib/layout/fit-rows.ts`); older layouts load with it off. The Document details section has a **Border around each field** option (with an optional border colour; empty uses the theme line colour) that boxes Invoice #, Date, Terms and the other fields individually; older layouts load with it off. Layout Builder's **Apply design to…** button copies the saved layout of the selected document type to any ticked document types in one save (a shortcut selects every type that prints with Layout Builder). Sections, positions, overlays/stamps, theme, custom text and “Use for printing” are copied; text naming the source document (for example “Tax Invoice” in the title or header name) is renamed for each target, and existing target layouts are replaced. Unsaved changes must be saved first.

Layout Builder documents show bilingual labels by default: English on the left and Arabic on the right for Bill To, Ship To, document details (number, date, due date, terms, sales rep, TRN), the document title (e.g. Tax Invoice / فاتورة ضريبية), item table headings (description, qty, rate, subtotal, VAT, amount and the other columns, Arabic under the English) and totals (subtotal, VAT, total, paid, balance). Toggle it with **Arabic labels** in Page & theme. An uploaded **AED currency symbol** image (for example the official UAE dirham sign) replaces "AED" in the totals of AED documents (`lib/layout/arabic-labels.ts`). The Report Center contains 124 report definitions with no duplicate entries: every category uses the same theme-token colours in light and dark mode, wide tables scroll sideways on desktop, and tables switch to labelled cards below 1024 px (tablet and phone). Money columns show negatives in red; identifiers, codes and years are shown without thousands separators. Every category, including Profit & Loss, places the movable company stamp after the final table in PDF output. For reports using the standard PDF layout, including customer and vendor statements, the stamp position guide draws the page where the table ends. It shows the real header, summary cards, rows and bank boxes, and puts the stamp where it will print, outlining your chosen spot when the stamp has to move below the table or onto a new page (`lib/report-stamp-preview.ts`). Inventory reports are grouped into valuation, availability/purchasing, and stock health/verification. A4 portrait/landscape PDF, CSV and Excel exports share the displayed filters; Excel includes a separate Inventory summary sheet. ### Shared Professional Report Center

The Report Center shows company/inventory context, accurate available-category counts, saved views and export formats. Report definitions are deduplicated by report key, retaining distinct reports with similar names. Desktop has a category sidebar; tablet/mobile have a compact category selector. Generic library cards use one open indicator. Supplier/currency options remain available when vendor reports appear in All Reports or search. Unknown or inaccessible saved report definitions are hidden.

Shared report tables use weighted desktop columns and labeled mobile/tablet cards with readable quantities and prices. Generic quantities retain up to six decimal places. Account links continue to use server-resolved account IDs and permission checks; ambiguous matches remain explicit review issues. Every report can use the optional A4 stamp preview with mouse, touch, keyboard or millimetre positioning; a single print stamp matches PDF coordinates. CSV and Excel now include available category summaries consistently with PDF. Generic exports use right-aligned numeric fields, wrapped Excel descriptions and A4 portrait/landscape tables. Employee balance amounts use the recorded employee currency on screen.

The generic table-export toolbar is suppressed inside Report Center dialogs, which already own PDF, Excel and CSV exports. This prevents duplicate export controls and keeps users on the report-aware output path that includes summaries, selected A4 orientation and stamps. Other transaction/table screens retain the fallback toolbar.

### Profit & Loss presentation and account reconciliation

The Profit & Loss library groups statements/comparisons, profitability analysis, and cost/account review. Reports use clear currency-labeled summary cards, section/subtotal/net-result styling, light/dark colors, and labeled mobile/tablet cards below 1024 px; A4 printing restores the complete table. A single export menu provides PDF, Excel and CSV. Account warnings are visible and included with the reporting basis and summary in every export; Excel also includes a formatted ledger-detail sheet. Detail and COGS reports avoid repeating the same ledger table below the main report. Existing shared stamp controls support mouse/touch dragging, arrow keys and millimetre offsets, with A4 portrait/landscape print and PDF placement.

Employees & HR reports are company-wide. **Employee Balance Summary** shows each employee's monthly salary, **paid in period** (non-cancelled cheques and expenses to the employee and employee loan payments in the report dates, home currency), last payment, the **loan / advance ledger balance** (loan account opening balance plus posted movement), manual loan, bank loans paid (cancelled excluded), and the salary expense, loan and paying bank accounts with codes; active employees are listed first. **Employee Payment Detail** lists non-cancelled cheques and expenses paid to employees and employee loan payments, with purpose (salary / payment, expense, loan / advance), the paying account and status. The company-wide Transaction Journal includes postings of documents saved under any inventory.

Lists reports: **Account Listing** shows each account's opening balance, posted movement and balance (home currency, normal side, company-wide), matching the Chart of Accounts. **Fixed Asset Listing** is company-wide, converts foreign opening balances, groups cost and accumulated-depreciation accounts (named like Accumulated Depreciation) and adds a **Net book value** line. **Item Price List**, **Price Level List** and **Item Listing** show the sales VAT code with price ex VAT and inc VAT (prices saved including VAT are split back out), the average purchase cost used by the stock reports (saved cost for non-stock items), margin and markup before VAT, the inventory, and for Item Listing the item type, income and COGS/purchase accounts; the price list keeps inactive items that still hold stock. Phone lists show currency and status; the customer contact list shows TRN. **Other Names** groups names case- and space-insensitively, shows document types and ignores cancelled and draft documents. **Terms Listing** uses the terms saved on documents (else derives them from the due date) and marks the source. **To Do Notes** and **Memorised Transactions** exclude voided, cancelled, draft and converted documents; overdue uses Dubai time.

Accountant reports: the **Trial Balance**, **General Ledger**, **Transaction Detail by Account**, **Journal** and **Transaction Journal** are company-wide. The Trial Balance shows each account's opening balance (saved on the account; foreign accounts at the current rate), posted debits and credits, and its closing balance on the normal side (Closing Debit / Closing Credit), with an equity line offsetting openings saved without a journal, so total debits equal total credits. Ledger running balances start from the opening balance, and Transaction Detail by Account adds a **Balance forward** row per account at the From date. **Voided/Deleted Transactions** include deleted documents and deleted manual journals (from the audit log, with number, type, party, user, reason and recorded amount) and voided or cancelled documents (with amount). **Transaction List by Date** shows each document's **Ledger effect** (Posted, Not posted for quotations/orders/pending invoices, Void / not posted) and totals posted documents only. **Customer Credit Card Audit Trail** shows charges positive and payments to the card negative. **Audit Trail** dates are filtered and shown in Dubai time with a **By** column. Document account columns link to verified Chart of Accounts entries.

Financial statements (Balance Sheet Standard/Detail/Summary/Prev Year, Net Worth Graph, Statement of Cash Flows, Cash Flow Forecast, Realised and Unrealised Gains & Losses) are company-wide; the selected inventory does not split them. Account opening balances (saved on the account in its currency) are included at home value (foreign accounts at the current rate): on each balance-sheet account, in opening and closing cash, and in the forecast's opening cash. Because those openings were saved without a journal, the balance sheet adds an equity line **Opening balances not posted to the ledger** so assets equal liabilities plus equity. **Expenses by Supplier** leaves out postings from sales documents (an invoice's cost of sales is not a supplier expense). The forecast and exchange reports treat credit notes and vendor credits linked to an invoice or bill as settling it, exclude deleted documents, match exchange rates case-insensitively, and flag foreign documents saved at exchange rate 1 instead of reporting a false gain or loss; Unrealised Gains & Losses is an as-of report. **Complete Business Final Report** uses the same bases as the corrected reports: net sales after returns, purchases without cancelled documents or bill-payment cheques, receivables and payables as balances at the period end, a company-wide **closing bank balance** including opening balances, and open orders that exclude cancelled and draft ones.

Customers (A/R) reports exclude cancelled, canceled, void, voided, deleted and draft documents (including customer statements). A sales receipt is listed in customer activity but has no receivable effect (it is paid on the spot), so cash sales no longer inflate customer balances, collections or the A/R graph. **Open Invoices** and **Collections Report** are as of the report's To date (today in Dubai when blank): older unpaid invoices stay listed, payments dated later or cancelled do not reduce the open amount, and today's "paid" status is not used. Open Invoices adds invoice total, paid, document-currency open amount, days overdue and aging band; Collections adds maximum days overdue and overdue amount and is sorted by overdue amount. A/R aging uses Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 91+; invoices without a due date age from the invoice date and count as overdue after it. Online Received Payments excludes void payments and shows method, reference and payment-currency amount. Customer Document Summary excludes cancelled and draft documents and counts quotations.

Vendors (A/P) reports exclude cancelled, canceled, void, voided, deleted and draft documents. **A/P Aging Summary/Detail** and **Unpaid Bills Detail** are as of the report's To date (today when blank): every bill dated on or before it is included whatever its date, payments and credits dated after it do not reduce the open amount, today's "paid" status does not hide a bill that was open then, and days past due are counted to the as-of date (a bill without a due date ages from its bill date). Buckets are Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 91+. Aging adds each supplier's **unapplied credits and payments** (unlinked vendor credits, payments on account, overpayments) so the aging total equals the supplier balance; Unpaid Bills shows bill total and paid/credited. **Supplier Balance Summary** and **A/P Aging Summary** show a ledger reconciliation of supplier balances to the Accounts Payable control account(s) in the report currency as of the report date, with the difference.

VAT reports and VAT Management exclude cancelled, canceled, void, voided, deleted and draft documents and stock-return lines, and round VAT to cents per line (as posting does). Bill freight is taxed once: when a bill saved its freight as a separate "Freight Charges" line (with its own VAT), the item line's freight is not added to its taxable value again, so neither the reports nor the VAT return (Box 9) overstate purchases or flag false calculation exceptions. **VAT Exceptions** also flags codes that are not a UAE treatment (anything other than 5%, zero, exempt or out of scope) and out-of-scope lines carrying VAT, matching VAT Management's filing checks. **VAT Position Summary** adds manual VAT adjustments (journals without a source document), net VAT after adjustments, and a reconciliation of output VAT to the VAT Payable ledger and input VAT to the Recoverable VAT ledger for the period, with the differences.

Sales reports share one set of posted sales: invoices and sales receipts, with credit memos as returns (negative); cancelled, canceled, void, voided, deleted and draft documents are excluded everywhere, and stock-return lines (negative quantity at zero price) are stock corrections, not sales. Amounts are home currency at each document's exchange rate, rounded to cents. Summaries (daily, customer, item, rep, ship-to) show Sales, Returns and Net Sales ex VAT with VAT and net total; detail reports list credit notes as negatives and show the document-currency amount. **Sales by Customer Summary** adds documents, currency, last sale and % of net sales; **Sales by Item Summary** adds returned quantity, average selling price (paid units only) and gross margin. **Sales by Ship To** groups by the document's delivery address (a country named in it, else its last line), falling back to the customer's country. **Pending Sales** is a current snapshot of quotations, estimates, proformas, pending invoices, the uninvoiced part of sales orders and the unpaid balance of invoices, with face value and open value. **Sales Order Fulfilment** is a current snapshot that ignores cancelled orders and invoices, counts fully converted orders as invoiced and shows the backlog (remaining quantity × price inc VAT).

Purchases reports share one set of purchase documents: bills, received item bills, expenses, cheques and credit card charges, with purchase returns (vendor credits) as negatives; cancelled, canceled, void, voided, deleted and draft documents are excluded everywhere. Amounts are home currency at each document's exchange rate. **Purchases by Supplier Summary** shows documents, last purchase, Purchases, Returns, Net Purchases ex VAT, VAT and Net Total; **Supplier Detail** adds the document-currency amount. **Purchases by Item Summary/Detail** net returns, list no "Freight Charges" item (line freight is added to the item's landed cost) and show freight, landed cost, average landed unit cost and last unit cost; Item Detail shows each line's **posted account** (stock asset account for stock items, otherwise the purchase/COGS or document account). A purchase return's payable column shows the payable account it debited. **Open Purchase Orders** reports are current snapshots (orders open today regardless of order date); receipts that were later cancelled no longer reduce the open quantity; orders with nothing left open are hidden, and **Days Past Expected** is shown. **Purchase Order Summary** separates Converted / Billed from Fully Received, counts drafts and cancellations as closed, and adds order value ex VAT.

Inventory reports value active stock-part items, plus inactive ones that still hold stock, at on-hand quantity × average purchase cost (bills and item receipts in home currency including line freight; cancelled, void and draft purchases excluded, also for the item average cost used on invoices). Items without a saved asset account use the INVENTORY system account, as posting does. Stock Valuation Summary/Detail, Stock Status and Stock Aging show a **Ledger reconciliation** panel: stock value per inventory asset account vs its posted ledger balance (plus opening balance when not limited to one inventory) and the difference, with account links. Stock Valuation Detail's stock value includes negative stock, matching the Summary. **Stock Status by Item** adds Inventory, On Hold (active stock holds), Available (on hand − held) and Unit Cost, and shows inactive items as Inactive. **Inventory Stock Aging** uses FIFO receipt layers (latest receipts first; older stock ages from item creation), shows unit cost, latest receipt, oldest stock date and its source, and an aging breakdown of quantity and value per band (0–30 … 366+ days). Stock Status by Supplier ignores cancelled purchases and breaks same-date ties by the latest document. Pending Builds lists active items at or below a positive reorder level. Negative Item List is a current snapshot.

Banking reports are company-wide (bank accounts belong to the company; the selected inventory does not filter them). **Bank Register** starts each bank account's running balance at its opening balance and shows amounts in the bank's own currency: home-currency banks use the home amount; a foreign-currency bank uses the original amount of postings in its currency and converts postings made in another currency at its current rate. Its closing card uses home values, with the opening balance at the current rate. **Bank Reconciliation** lists every posted line on a bank account, including sales receipts, expenses, vendor payments and manual journals ("journal entry"), so it adds up to the bank's ledger movement; Cleared is taken from the source document's status.

**Cost of sales** for a stock item is what it was bought for: the weighted average of its supplier bills and item receipts (quantity × unit price + line freight at each document's exchange rate, home currency; cancelled, void and draft purchases excluded), the same figure the Items list shows as average cost (`lib/item-cost.ts`). New invoice lines, invoices converted from sales orders, stock-return lines and invoice item changes use it, falling back to the last purchase price and then the item's saved Cost field (ex VAT) only when the item has no purchases. The saved Cost field is not updated by bills, so it is no longer used when purchases exist.

Profit & Loss reports read posted journal amounts in home currency: invoices and sales receipts credit each item's income account at line subtotal × exchange rate and debit its COGS account at quantity × unit cost × exchange rate; bills debit the inventory asset (stock items) or COGS/purchase account. Manual journals in another currency are converted when saved; the original amount is kept separately. The cost-of-sales review warning only flags stock items sold with no positive unit cost, not services. **Item Profitability** shows Sales ex VAT, Cost of sales and Gross Profit per item from invoice and sales-receipt lines at each document's exchange rate, excluding void, voided, cancelled, canceled and draft documents. **Stock Pricing & Profit/Loss** converts each supplier bill at its own exchange rate.

Profit & Loss by Customer groups posted income and cost of sales by the customer on the saved invoice, sales receipt, credit memo, statement or finance charge; postings without a customer document (bills, operating expenses, manual journals) stay in Unallocated. Each customer name opens the Sales register filtered to that customer.

It opens the item's sales invoices and sales receipts in the period (date, invoice, customer, qty, unit selling price, unit cost, sales, cost, profit) and its bills, received item bills and item receipts up to the period end (latest 25; qty, unit cost, amount), all in home currency at each document's exchange rate, with references that open the document. Data: `report.pnl.itemActivity` from `lib/profit-loss.ts`.

Include account lines switches exports to detailed postings while preserving totals. File names include company, period and download date. A4 portrait is the default, with landscape available. Shared company-stamp controls support mouse/touch dragging, arrow keys and millimetre placement; print/PDF use that position on the first page.

Desktop uses aligned list columns; tablets and phones use labeled cards and expandable postings. Journal entry amount fields also reflow. Light/dark colors follow the app theme, red delete actions stay permission-controlled, and explicit Minimize/Restore preserves list filters and dialog drafts.

## Income tax reporting

Report Library → Financial → Income Tax Working Paper provides editable percentage bands, threshold, tax period, additions/deductions and explanations. The AED preset uses 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above it; Flat rate sets threshold to zero. Non-AED companies start with zero rates/threshold and require custom configuration. The threshold is applied once to the selected period, without proration. This is an estimate: special regimes, exemptions, reliefs, loss utilisation, credits and top-up tax require separate review. Rate reference: https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-confirms-applicable-taxable-income-threshold-for-corporate-tax-abu-dhabi-uae/ (checked 2026-10-04).

Posted ledger income less COGS/expenses gives accounting profit. The selected tax-expense account is added back to derive profit before income tax, followed by manual adjustments. Negative adjusted profit produces zero current estimated taxable income, not an automatic tax-loss carry-forward. Summaries and detailed calculations use home currency across all company inventories. Verified account links open history; missing/ambiguous links are flagged. A provision comparison identifies the selected expense/payable accounts without automatically posting anything.

Administrators/accountants can create/link the standard tax accounts, save working papers and update saved periods. One configuration per company/date range prevents duplicates and revision checks prevent overwriting another user's changes. Report readers may preview and export. Saved configurations recalculate from the live ledger; they are not filed-return snapshots. Clear company → saved reports includes working papers.

Responsive calculation rows, light/dark styling, shared Minimize/Restore dialog controls, A4 portrait/landscape print/PDF, CSV and Excel, report summaries and draggable/touch/keyboard stamp placement share the same calculation. Exports require recalculation after settings change, include company/period/date in filenames, and show assumptions and review notes. Print opens the A4 PDF in a printable browser tab; stamps are on the first PDF page.

Customer/vendor/banking action cards use the shared theme. Register-owned exports suppress redundant generic toolbar exports, while shared numeric table fields retain responsive widths and alignment. ### Customer and vendor sub-accounts

## Register row actions

In transaction registers (Sales, Purchases and others) the row actions sit on one line in fixed slots (view, attachments, delivery, edit, delete), so each icon lines up down the column; a slot stays empty when that action does not apply to the row. ## Stock holds

Administrators and accountants can put stock on hold for a sales rep from Inventory Overview: **Hold** on a product opens a form for the inventory, quantity (up to the free stock), duration (4 hours, 1 day, 2 days or custom up to 7 days), the sales rep (an active employee) and a note. The same form lists the product's current holds on the chosen inventory (quantity, sales rep, note, time left) with **Unhold** to release one early. Inventory Overview shows a **Stock on hold** panel with time left and **Release**, an "on hold" badge on the product and "on hold · free" under its quantity. While a hold is active, only documents for that sales rep may use the held quantity: invoices, sales receipts and invoice edits by other reps, purchase returns and stock transfers (unless for the same rep) are blocked with a clear message, even with the admin negative-stock override. On a new invoice or sales receipt, choosing a held product fills in only the free quantity, the product list shows "on hold" next to its stock, and the line shows how many are on hold, for which sales rep and for how long; asking for more turns the note red and saving is refused. Holds end automatically when they expire; releases and creations are audited, and holds are included in company backups.

## Inventory Overview filters and price adjustment

Besides search and the top filter bar (All / per-inventory / In stock / Low stock / **Out of stock** for products with no stock in any inventory / **On hold** / **Received 7 days** / **30 days**), Inventory Overview filters by **Category**, **Brand** and **Selling price (excl. VAT)** range, with Clear filters. Brand is a well-known brand found in the item name, then its description or specifications, otherwise the first word of the name. **Price +** adds a percentage or a fixed amount to the prices shown and shared (WhatsApp, Telegram, email and Excel, whose price column notes the increase), on top of which Including VAT still applies; saved selling prices never change.
