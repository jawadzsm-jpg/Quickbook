# Features and functionality

## Company workspace

- Multiple independent companies with home currency, legal identity, TRN, address, contact, and bank details.
- Multiple inventories per company, each with its own code, invoice prefix, and next invoice number.
- **Warehouses inside an inventory** (Management → Inventories, Phase 1): each inventory card lists its warehouses with item and unit counts. Administrators add, rename, set the default, deactivate (empty warehouses only) and remove (empty, non-default) warehouses. Every inventory has a default "Main warehouse"; stock not placed in another warehouse is counted there, so the inventory total is unchanged. Changing the default moves no stock. **Phase 2:** bills, item receipts, invoices, sales receipts and purchase returns post to a warehouse. When an inventory has more than one active warehouse, these forms show a **Warehouse** picker next to Inventory (default selected); otherwise nothing changes. The negative-stock check (and admin PIN override) uses the chosen warehouse's quantity; edits, invoice voids and deletes put stock back into the warehouse it came from and are refused when that warehouse no longer has it. Saved invoices keep their warehouse; bill/receipt/return edits keep theirs unless another is chosen. Transfers between inventories take from and add to each inventory's default warehouse and cannot take more than the default warehouse holds. A warehouse used by any document or stock history cannot be removed (deactivate it instead). **Phase 3:** Stock Transfers has a **Move between warehouses** section for the active inventory (shown when it has two or more active warehouses): choose From/To, add products with their quantity in the From warehouse, an optional reference and notes; recent moves are listed with **Reverse** (Administrator; refused when the destination no longer has the stock). Moves change only warehouse stock — the inventory total, cost and accounts stay the same. The Items list shows each item's per-warehouse quantities under its description, and Inventory Overview shows warehouse tags (e.g. "Showroom 1") beside the inventory and a **Warehouse** filter that shows only stock in that warehouse with its quantity — both only for inventories with more than one warehouse. With the Warehouse filter on, the WhatsApp/Telegram/Email copy buttons (and Edit before copying) and the Excel export use that warehouse's quantity and leave out selected items with no stock there; the success message says how many were left out. The Excel export also adds a Warehouse column. The copied text does not name the warehouse. **Print**, with its **Portrait / Landscape** select, sits on the "Consolidated stock" heading line next to search and prints an A4 stock list of the selected items, or every listed item when none are selected. Its heading gives the company, the warehouse (or All warehouses), the item count and the print time. It has item no., SKU, item, specifications, inventory, the warehouse when filtered, and quantity and price unless they are hidden (in the chosen VAT format), with a total-quantity row. Quantities follow the Warehouse filter. A warehouse with documents, stock history or moves cannot be removed. **Stock reports** (Inventory Valuation Summary/Detail, Inventory Stock Status by Item/Supplier, Stock Aging, Negative Item List, Physical Inventory Worksheet, Pending Builds, Warehouse Movements, Stock Transfers) show a **Warehouse** selector beside "Hide zero QOH" when a reported inventory has two or more warehouses. Choosing one re-runs the report with only that warehouse's quantities and value (the header shows "Inventory · Warehouse"). The ledger reconciliation is hidden while a warehouse is selected, because ledger balances cover whole inventories. Stock aging assigns the warehouse's quantity to the newest receipt layers of the item.
- Company and inventory selectors that scope forms, lists, dashboard data, and most reports.
- Per-user company assignments, global administration, company administration, and six operational roles.
- **Dashboard** is the first link in the sidebar, above the Overview section (Administrators, Accountants, Purchasing, Inventory and Viewers; it replaced the former Company Home → Insights tab). See [Business dashboard](#business-dashboard).
- **Sidebar sections are dropdowns:** each heading (Overview, Customers, Vendors, Inventory, Accountant, Management) opens and closes its section with a click (chevron). **Overview** starts open on every page until the user closes it; the other sections start closed. **Inventory** ends with Warranty / RMA. **Accountant** holds Receive Payment, Banking, General Journal, Chart of Accounts, VAT Management, RCM Declaration, Employees & HR and Reports; each role sees only the pages it already could, and a section with none of its pages is hidden. The section of the page being shown opens on its own; the others stay as the user left them (kept in this browser). With the sidebar collapsed to icons every item shows.
- **My notes** (sidebar → Overview → **My notes**, every role): each user's private notepad for the selected company — add a note (Enter adds, Shift+Enter makes a new line), tick it done (done notes move to the bottom, struck through), edit or delete it. Up to 1,000 characters per note and 100 notes per company; nobody else, administrators included, sees them.
- **Announcements** (sidebar → Overview → **Announcements**, every role): Administrators (their companies) and All-Admins post an announcement to **all users of the company** or **chosen users**, with an optional end date. Each recipient sees it as a card on the Announcements page until they press **Got it**, and as a notice in the bell (with an email copy when notification emails are on). Administrators can edit, end or remove announcements and see how many people have seen each one (**Announcements** / **New announcement** on that page).
- **Two-step sign-in** (optional, per user): in the key button's **Account security** window → **Two-step sign-in**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy…), enter the first code, and save the 10 one-time backup codes. Sign-in then asks for the 6-digit code after the password (a backup code also works, once). Users can get new backup codes or turn it off (password and code required). Administrators see "Two-step on" in Users & access and can **Reset two-step** for someone who lost their phone.
- **Change password** for every user (key button next to Sign out at the bottom of the sidebar): enter the current password and a new one (12–128 characters, with a strength hint and show/hide); every session is signed out and the user signs in again. The window points to **Forgot password?** for users who no longer know their current password.
- **Sign-in when the server is down**: if the server crashes or cannot be reached (for example the database is over its plan quota), the sign-in page says "The server is unavailable right now. Please try again in a few minutes." instead of a technical JSON error.
- **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page: users enter their email and receive a one-use reset link (valid 30 minutes) through the configured email server, then choose a new password. Without SMTP settings, users are told to ask their administrator.
- **EasyBook edition** (Admin Controls → EasyBook edition): sets how many companies, inventories and warehouses this EasyBook may use — **Multi company** (many of each, the default), **Single company** (one company, many inventories and warehouses), **Single company · one inventory** (many warehouses) or **Single company · one warehouse**. Only an All-Admin changes it; other administrators see it. Switching to a smaller edition is refused while more companies, inventories or warehouses are active than it allows, with a list of what to remove first; nothing is hidden or deleted. Under a smaller edition the Add company, Add inventory and Add warehouse controls are replaced by a note, and the APIs refuse extra records (including reactivating a warehouse).
- **Top logo on the sign-in page**: Company Setup → Login page → **Top logo** shows the EasyBook logo or an uploaded replacement (PNG/JPG/WebP up to 500 KB; **Use EasyBook logo** goes back), **Remove (hide) the top logo**, and **Swap: show the company logo first** (the upper image and company logo above the top logo). Columns `login_top_logo_data`, `login_top_logo_hidden`, `login_logos_swapped` (migration 0129); companies without their own use the default login company's. **Top logo position** moves it up or down (Up/Down 10 px, or type −150 to 150 px; Reset; Save position), with the preview below. Companies without their own position use the default login company's. Column `companies.login_logo_offset` (migration 0128), saved through `PATCH /api/company-setup/branding` (`field: "loginLogoOffset"`).
- **Automatic sign-out when idle**: a user who does not use the keyboard, mouse, touch screen or scrolling for 15 minutes (default) is signed out. A countdown appears for the final minute with **Stay signed in** and **Sign out**; the sign-in page then says why. Open tabs share activity, so working in one tab keeps the others signed in. Administrators choose each user's time (5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or Never) in Admin Controls → Users & access, on each user row and when adding a user; the change does not sign the user out. The server applies the same limit, so a session left on a sleeping computer also ends.
- Configurable login branding, company logos, background, display name, copyright text, document colors, templates, and letterhead.
- Light and dark appearance modes plus a per-user accent colour (Emerald, Ocean, Indigo, Violet, Rose or Amber), both chosen in the header Appearance menu and saved to the user's account, plus a live header panel, available to every role including Sales, showing online users with their last-activity date and time (UAE) on one line; only administrators also see sign-in IP addresses.

## Sales and customers

- Customer Center: compact workflow cards (icon beside title, one-line description from tablet width; 2 columns on phones, 3 on laptops, 4 on very wide screens) so the customer list is reached quickly; the contacts table (shared with Vendor Center and Employees) keeps names readable, phone and balance on one line, and a compact Make inactive / Activate button. On wide screens the sales-user assignment and the customer balance summary share one row. In the contacts table (Customer, Vendor and Employee lists) the Currency column shows from 1,280 px; below that the balance already carries the currency, so the table fits a 1,120 px screen, and row actions use 32 px icons.
- Sales & Invoicing: a workflow panel (quotation/estimate → proforma → sales order → invoice, minimisable) above one toolbar of document-type pills (All sales, Quotations, Pending Cart, Estimates, Proforma Invoices, Sales Orders, Invoices) with the void Status filter; the register has search with a one-line date range, A4/stamp/export tools, and a transactions table (shared with Purchases and Banking) whose date and amount stay on one line and whose action icons are never cut off. The workflow panel uses the same compact step cards as Purchases & Bills: the four steps in one row from laptop width and two per row on phones (descriptions from tablet width), with the ledger note beside the Create pending invoice and Chart of Accounts buttons.

- Customer records with company, international phone/WhatsApp validation, country, reseller/planet fields, TRN, email, credit, and currency.
- Quotations, estimates, proforma invoices, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, statement charges, finance charges, and credit memos.
- Estimate, proforma invoice, sales order, and invoice entry forms offer Add Line below the last item row so long documents can grow without scrolling to the top. Their posting-account dropdown defaults to the selected customer’s linked Accounts Receivable account in the document currency.
- Partial conversion of estimates, proformas, and sales orders into one or more invoices with source-line allocation tracking.
- Customer payment entry against one or multiple open invoices, including partial allocations and refreshed invoice status.
- Deleting an invoice voids it: it stays in Sales with status Void, its item history and a stock-return movement are kept, its journal is unposted, and the row shows who voided it, the date and time (Dubai) and the reason. The Sales **Status** filter shows Not void (default), Void invoices, or All, including void. Void invoices cannot be edited, delivered, voided again or given new or edited packing lists (saved packing lists stay viewable and printable); their document view hides Ship To, Mark as delivered and inventory item changes and shows who voided it, when and why, and appear in the Voided/Deleted Transactions report.
- Customer center with balances, activity, open balances, statements, overdue analysis, and source document drill-down. Its **Customer workflows** panel offers quotations, estimates, proforma invoices, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, payments, credit notes and the customer reports; statement and finance charges are not in that panel (they remain available from Home).
- A customer or supplier statement for one party prints that party's postal address and `TRN …` under **Statement for** (from the contact's saved address and TRN); statements for all customers or vendors show only the heading.
- Customer and supplier statements can include a movable bank details box: the Company Setup bank account for the statement currency (bank, account name, number, IBAN, SWIFT, currency) and its remark, editable for that statement. It prints after the statement table at the chosen left offset in screen print, standard PDF and custom-template PDF output, with the company stamp placed after it.
- Customer and supplier statements use the letterhead assigned to **Statement** in every output. Screen print shows its header (logos, heading, contact line) and footer, prints customer and reference cells as plain text, leaves out the on-screen reporting-basis and summary panels (the statement heading carries the totals), stays light in dark mode, and prints the company stamp after the table and bank boxes at its Left offset. The A4 PDF export draws the same letterhead header and footer on every page with a "Statement of Account" summary, a "Statement for … · period" line and a light table; the stamp position guide shows that header. Without an assigned letterhead the generic report PDF layout is used.
- Payment terms and calculated due dates. **Due on receipt** is the default on every customer document: new documents, invoices converted from quotations, estimates, proformas and sales orders without terms, and older documents opened for editing with blank terms. The server saves blank customer-document terms as Due on receipt; bills and other vendor documents are unchanged.

## Purchasing and vendors

- Vendor records with contact, company, country, TRN, currency, payable balance, and change history.
- Purchase orders, item receipts, received-item bills, bills, expenses, vendor credits, and bill payments. Purchases & Bills shows the five purchase steps (order → receive → bill → pay → return) in one row from laptop width (2 columns on phones), then one toolbar of list pills (All purchases, Bills, Purchase Orders, Item Receipts, Purchase Returns) with the PO status filter on the same line, as in Sales & Invoicing. Transaction lists use compact 32 px row-action icons, and long company/inventory tags wrap, so the register fits a 1,120 px screen. On wide screens the Purchase Order Summary shortcut and the Bill of Entry search share a row; once a Bill of Entry search shows results, both take the full width.
- Supplier bills using the `Goods imported into the UAE` VAT code require Bill of Entry and Airway Bill references, support customs-document attachments, and keep those references on the saved and printed bill. When converting an import-coded Purchase Order, the UAE import document fields are shown immediately and become required as soon as an import line is included in the Bill. Purchases includes a company- and inventory-scoped Bill of Entry No. search whose results open the original supplier bill.
- **Reverse charge and imports (UAE self-assessment):** on a bill, received-item bill, expense, cheque or credit card charge, the VAT on REVERSE_CHARGE (or RCM / REVERSE) lines, and on every line of a bill marked imported or using Goods imported into the UAE, is not owed to the supplier. It posts Dr Recoverable VAT / Cr VAT Payable, and Accounts Payable (or the bank / card) is credited with the rest only. The document keeps its VAT amount for VAT reports and the VAT201 (Boxes 3, 6 and 10), and its **total is the amount payable** (net), which drives the supplier balance, open bills and payments. A purchase return reverses both sides (Dr VAT Payable / Cr Recoverable VAT) and takes the import flag of its bill. The bill form shows the self-assessed VAT and **Amount due to supplier**. Import VAT is always self-assessed; VAT paid at customs belongs on the customs agent's bill, with any correction typed in Box 7. `selfAssessedPurchaseVat` in `lib/vat-return.ts` is the shared rule. Documents saved before this change keep their old posting (AP credited with net + VAT, nothing in VAT Payable): open and save such a bill (the edit reposts it; open bills in an unfiled period only) or post a journal Dr Accounts Payable / Cr VAT Payable for the VAT; bills with payments must have the payments removed before editing. Converting a purchase order marks the bill imported only when the order was an import.
- Bills and purchase orders default their posting-account dropdown to the selected vendor’s linked Accounts Payable account in the document currency.
- Purchase order and bill entry forms offer Add Line below the last item row.
- Partial purchase-order receiving with remaining quantities and receipt allocation tracking.
- Purchase returns constrained to quantities still available from source bills.
- Single or multi-bill vendor payment allocation and refreshed bill status.
- Vendor center with balances, purchasing history, returns, open payables, aging, and vendor-currency reports. Supplier workflows use the same compact cards as Customer Center (2 columns on phones, 4 from laptop width); in the vendor list, Edit and Delete wrap under the vendor name when the table is narrow, so the table fits a 1,120 px screen without sideways scrolling.
- Cargo List (sidebar, under Vendors): a shared list of cargo and freight agents for the company, with name, telephone, mobile, email, modes (Air, Sea, Land, Courier), destination countries, address and notes. Every user role can view it and add entries; the person who added an entry, or an administrator, can edit it; only administrators delete. It has search, page size and paging, a desktop table and mobile cards. The table shows from 1,280 px and fits the screen without sideways scrolling (phones and actions stay on one line, emails wrap after the @); narrower screens show cards, two per row from tablet width.
- Supplier bank details (bank name, account name, account number, IBAN, SWIFT/BIC) on each vendor, entered in the new-vendor form or Edit, shown on the Vendor Center supplier profile, and printable as a movable box on purchase orders, bills and single-vendor Vendor Statements.
- Supplier Centre Quick report and Open balance use the selected vendor’s currency, including foreign-currency vendors in a company with a different home currency.

## Inventory

- Stock, non-stock, service, other charge, subtotal, group, discount, payment, VAT item, and VAT group item types.
- Unique item numbers and SKU controls per inventory location.
- Quantity on hand, reorder points, sales prices, unit cost, GRN cost, serial numbers, and active status.
- Inventory items load independently from transactions, contacts, and account balances; historical average cost and open-PO quantities are aggregated in PostgreSQL, and long item lists are paginated for responsive browsing.
- **Inventory Overview email lists:** the same lists also have their own page, **Email lists** in the sidebar under Overview (after My notes; every role that can use a list: Admins, All-Admins, Accountants, Sales, Purchasing and Inventory users): it shows the lists, categories, owners and the email form as on the Send dialog without any stock selected, and sending clears the selection instead of closing. The **Send** button (next to the Email copy button; every role except Viewer, Sales included) emails the selected items to chosen addresses of the company's **Sales list** (customers) or **Purchase list** (suppliers). The two lists are kept separately, and each user's addresses are private to them (the same address may be in several users' lists); **Admins and All-Admins** see, use and remove every user's addresses in the company, with who added each. Sales users see and use the Sales list only. Administrators choose whose list is open with **List of**: **My list** (the default), **All users** (each address shows whose list it is in) or one user; addresses added while a user's list is open go into that user's list. Each address has an optional name, company (migration 0132) and **category** (migration 0134) — a country, region or any label the user types, suggested from the categories already used. The **Category** filter beside the search shows one category (or those with none), with counts; **Select shown** then selects that category, e.g. to send to every UAE contact. **Set category** (a dropdown of the categories, or No category to clear) puts the selected addresses in a category. To fill a category with addresses already in the list, use the list icon beside it in the Categories panel, or **Choose addresses to add** when an empty category is shown: the list then shows the addresses not in it, to tick and **Add** (Send and Remove wait until this is done or cancelled). While a category is shown, a new address typed in the form goes into it. The **Categories** button opens a panel to add a category before any address uses it, rename one (its addresses follow; renaming into an existing name merges them) or delete one (after a confirmation its addresses stay in the list without a category). Categories are saved per user and list (migration 0135); an administrator manages the open user's categories, and with All users open renaming and deleting apply to every user's list. Addresses are added by hand (Name, Company name, Email, Category; the category is kept for the next address) or imported from a CSV file (an Email column and optionally Name/Contact, Company and Category/Country/Region/Group columns, or one address per row with the name, company and category in the next cells; rows without a category take the one being filtered; comma or semicolon; duplicates and invalid rows skipped), searched (name, email or category), edited (the pencil beside an address changes its name, company, category or email; an address already in the list is refused), selected (or all shown) and removed. With no item selected, **Send** still opens: the dialog becomes a plain **Email** starting from a blank message ("News from {company}") for news or items from the company website, and `{items}` in a template is left out. The standard wording greets each recipient by name: `Dear {name},` is filled per address with its name from the list (Sir/Madam without one); the company is not shown (`{contactCompany}` adds it in a template that wants it). When the subject or message uses `{name}` or `{contactCompany}`, each address gets its own copy addressed to it alone (one send per address); otherwise the email goes as hidden batches. The message starts from the standard stock email or a **stock list** email template (Sales users can save their own; `{items}`, `{company}`, `{sender}`, `{senderEmail}`) with the selected items in the email copy format. The sent email colours the selected items without changing their text: the item name and SKU blue, bold and underlined, the condition (e.g. BRAND NEW) and "Price: …" red, the specifications bold, and the dashed separators grey (an item line edited in the dialog is sent plain). Recipients are Bcc'd in batches of 50 so they never see each other; replies go to the sender; there is no limit on how many addresses an email goes to or a list holds: a larger selection is sent in parts of 500 one after another with progress (each address once, even when it is in several users' lists; if the email server stops part-way, the message says how many were already sent), up to 200 parts (100,000 addresses) per user per hour; the email server's own sending limits still apply. CSV imports of any number of rows (files up to 50 MB) are added in parts of 2,000, and a list longer than 1,000 addresses shows the first 1,000 (search or a category narrows it; Select all still selects every match). Table `email_list_contacts` (migration 0127; private per user since migration 0130; categories since migrations 0134–0135; `email_list_categories`), API `/api/email-lists` and `/api/inventory-emails`.
- In the Inventory list and on each Inventory Overview product line (the trailing #Item No.), resting the mouse on an Item No. for a moment copies it to the clipboard (with a "copied" notice); clicking it, or Enter/Space when focused, also copies it, for browsers such as Safari that only allow copying after a click. Inventory Overview share emoji: next to the WhatsApp and Telegram copy buttons each user picks an emoji style (Classic, Hot deal, Star, Ready stock, Premium, Rocket or No emoji), saved to their account per channel (`lib/share-emoji.ts`). The style marks the product title and condition and the screen, quantity and price lines; Classic keeps the previous WhatsApp and Telegram text. **Edit** in the share bar opens the message (WhatsApp, Telegram or Email format, with the user's emoji style) in a text box: users change any words for that one message, can **Reset text** or switch format, then **Copy**; edits are not saved. A WhatsApp-style formatting bar above the text box applies Bold, Italic, Strikethrough and Code to the selected words (`*bold*`, `_italic_`, `~strike~`, `` `code` `` for WhatsApp; `**bold**`, `__italic__`, `~~strike~~`, `` `code` `` for Telegram) and Numbered list, Bulleted list and Quote to the selected lines (`1. `, `- `, `> `); pressing a button again removes the formatting. Email shows only the list and quote buttons (`lib/share-format.ts`). Administrators switch share emoji on or off per company in Admin Controls → Security (**Share emoji**); when it is off, that company's products are shared without emoji and the style pickers are hidden if no listed company allows them. Inventory Overview product lines are plain text (Product Customization Details opens only from the row's **Details** button). The product's part number copies the same way as the #Item No.: its Part Number field (else its Part Number / MPN specification) when written in the line, otherwise the part-number code found in the line itself (the last "|" section that is one 6+ character code with letters and digits, such as 90NB1603-M002S0, skipping sizes like 16GB and resolutions like 1920X1200; `partNumberInLine` in `lib/item-description.ts`). A saved part number that is not in the line shows as a "Part No." chip under it.
- The record editor window (items, customers, vendors, accounts and transactions) shows **Save changes** / **Save record** in its top bar next to minimize, full screen and close, as well as at the bottom; both submit the same form (`controlBarActions` on `DialogContent`).
- Authorized inventory editors can mark each item inactive or active from its row. Inactive items move out of All items into the Item is inactive tab, and reactivated items return to All items. The inactive status label applies even at zero quantity; active stock counts exclude inactive items. Active items with zero (or negative) quantity on hand move out of All items into the **Out of stock** tab and return to All items once stock is received; the separate **Out of stock · Other companies** tab lists catalogue items not yet in the selected inventory. While the search box has text, All items also shows out-of-stock (and low-stock) matches, so a search never hides an active item; its count follows. Inactive items stay in their own tab and are pointed to by the "only inactive matches" notice. An X inside the search box (shown only when it has text) clears the search and returns to page 1.
- **Unused items become inactive automatically**: the five-minute scheduler makes an active item inactive when it has no stock on hand (zero quantity) and nothing has used it for **90 days** — no document line of any type (by the document's entry time), warehouse or company stock transfer, stock hold, Today Offer, inventory check, or item audit entry (edit, price change, revaluation, status change) since then, and it was created more than 90 days ago. Items with stock (positive or negative), an unexpired hold, a running or scheduled offer, or a line on a sales order, purchase order or pending invoice that is still open are never changed. Each change is written to the audit log (`auto-inactivated`, with the last-used time and reason). Reactivate an item from the Item is inactive tab (or from a search hint below); the reactivation is audited and gives the item a fresh 90 days (`lib/item-idle.ts`).
- **Inactive items in searches**: when a search by part number (Part Number field, Part Number / MPN specification or the code in the product line), SKU, item no. or UPC matches an inactive item, the item list (any tab except Item is inactive), Inventory Overview and the product pickers on bills, estimates, quotations, sales orders and other document lines say **"You have this item — it is in Inactive items."** with the item's name, item no., SKU and part number. **Open** shows it in the item list's Item is inactive tab (from Inventory Overview it opens the item's inventory); **Reactivate** (roles that manage inventory) makes it active again, and the picker or list reloads so it can be used at once. Whole codes or one word of the search count (at least 3 characters; 4+ characters may be part of a longer code), ignoring case, spaces and dashes (`lib/inactive-item-match.ts`, `app/inactive-item-notice.tsx`).
- Item specifications with reusable option values and generated descriptions.
- **Duplicate item** copies only the item's description and specifications (with its category, type, accounts and VAT codes) into an unsaved draft with a new SKU and item number. Everything else starts empty for you to fill: opening quantity 0 (enforced by the server), cost 0 (enforced), sales price 0, reorder point 0, no preferred supplier and no open-PO quantity.
- The Items list **Avg. cost** column shows the average purchase cost from the item's own bills and item receipts (falling back to the last purchase price, then the saved Cost when it has none), the same figure used for cost of sales.
- Inventory Center items list fits a 1,120 px screen: below 1,280 px the category pill shows under the item description instead of in its own Category column, cells use compact padding, and the duplicate/edit/delete icons are 32 px. From 1,280 px the separate Category column returns.
- Inventory item rows show one uppercase line ordered as brand/model, generated SKU, condition such as `BRAND NEW`, remaining specifications, and the item number as a final `#` suffix. Generated and displayed descriptions omit every standalone `NO` specification. When the **Model** specification is `NO` (the item has no model), Inventory Overview leaves the trailing `NO` of the saved name and the generated SKU out of the product line, the WhatsApp/Telegram/email copies and the Edit box, and out of the Name and SKU columns of Print and Excel; the item editor's Generated description preview follows the same rule. The saved name, SKU and description are unchanged. Inventory Overview email, WhatsApp, and Telegram copies retain that complete description but leave out the `#` Item No.; social copies use the same `🔺` product-title and `⚡` condition format for Brand New, Open Box, Refurbished, and Used items while omitting inventory-location lines.
- HS code, country of origin, dimensions, and weight maintenance. Each item is a card with HS code, COO and dimension beside it; length/width/height/weight are two per row on phones and four from tablet width, and the header shows the item and unsaved counts before the Minimize, Reset and Save Changes actions.
- Inventory **Import** (users who can add items): upload Excel (.xlsx, Excel XML / HTML .xls such as the app's own exports) or CSV into the selected inventory. A header row with Name / Item / Item & description / Description is required; SKU, Item No., Category, Quantity (opening quantity, same as Add new), Reorder point, Sales price, Cost, Status and Type map to item fields, and any other named column becomes a specification. Prices such as `AED 6,000.00` are accepted. The dialog previews and flags bad rows, then saves each row through `POST /api/records` like Add new (generated SKUs/item numbers, unique-name check) and lists the result per row. Up to 2,000 rows per file; old binary .xls files must be re-saved as .xlsx or CSV. .xls markup (Excel XML or HTML tables) is read as plain text, never parsed as a web page. A CSV template is downloadable (`lib/item-import.ts`, `app/item-import.tsx`).

## Filed period lock

Admins and accountants can enable and move the company stamp. Counts avoid combining order, receipt, bill and payment values as if they were expenses. The loaded register is limited to recent transactions; authorized users can open Purchase Order Summary for broader reporting. Source order selection and receiving use responsive cards with quantities and original unit prices. Existing accounting, stock, import-document and permission rules remain in force.

### Document controls and clear stamp placement

Document previews group layout choices, copy selection and print/PDF actions into responsive sections with theme-aware colors. Record registers separate search/date filters from output actions. On transaction previews (classic templates and Layout Builder layouts) the company stamp and a bank details box can be placed anywhere on the first page: drag them or type Left / Top in millimetres from the top-left of the printable area. The bank details box prints, as text, the Company Setup bank account matching the document currency (default account and every extra account) with that account's remark and optional bank stamp image; the remark can be rewritten for a single document. Company Setup accepts any number of extra bank accounts in any currency, including several in one currency. When a currency has more than one account (the default account counts), tick **Print this account on XXX documents** on the one to use. With none ticked, the default account prints for its own currency, otherwise the first account listed in that currency. On a single sales document (quotation, proforma, sales order, invoice, sales receipt, credit memo), accountants and administrators can pick any of the company's bank accounts in the preview (**Bank account on this document**: one button per account plus **Automatic**, each showing the bank, currency and the last digits of the account; next to the bank box controls, shown when the company has more than one account). The choice is saved on the document and used by every template, Layout Builder output and its packing list / HS summary; **Automatic** returns to the currency rule. The same **Bank account** field is on the sales forms (quotation, estimate, proforma, sales order, invoice, sales receipt, credit memo, and the invoice details edit) for accountants and administrators, so the account can be chosen when the document is created or edited. An invoice made from a quotation, estimate, proforma or sales order (full conversion or partial invoicing) starts with the bank account chosen on its source. If a chosen account is later removed from Company Setup, the document falls back to the currency rule. A Layout Builder "{status}" stamp (OPEN, PAID, …) can also be moved, hidden and saved as a Status stamp; the template's own bank block follows the template design. Both stamps start switched off on every document type until ticked, and Save stamp positions stores their on/off state and position per document type. Standard report PDFs place stamps after the last table, adding a page when necessary and preserving footer clearance. Custom template body fields and accounting values are unchanged.

### Shared workspace arrangement

All shared dialogs use a dedicated control bar for minimize, maximize/restore and close, with consistent touch targets and light/dark colors. Controls remain outside the document content and are hidden in print. Minimized dialogs retain a visible title and restore controls.

Search is word-based across the app's main record lists (Inventory, customers, vendors, sales and purchase registers), Inventory Overview and Shortcuts: every typed word must appear, in any order and any case (`lib/search.ts`). Every search box (placeholder starting “Search”, `type=search` or `data-search`) highlights its words in amber inside its own results, in light and dark mode, using the CSS Custom Highlight API so lists are never re-rendered (`app/search-highlighter.tsx`, mounted in the root layout). The app header Shortcuts button opens a searchable, categorized list derived from the current user's permitted sidebar pages. Ctrl/Command+K opens it when no dialog is active; selecting a page preserves the active company. Shortcut IDs are deduplicated. Every user sees a live UAE date and time (weekday, date, time on one line, refreshed every 15 seconds) just before the Shortcuts button. A compact calculator icon on the same header row opens a light/dark-mode keypad for quick arithmetic without leaving the current workspace. Purchase order, bill, invoice, quotation, estimate, proforma invoice and sales order editors also have a Calculator button in their header; that calculator stays open while the document form is used and can copy its result for pasting into a field. Customer/vendor/banking action cards use the shared theme. Register-owned exports suppress redundant generic toolbar exports, while shared numeric table fields retain responsive widths and alignment. Existing structured A4/PDF/CSV/Excel reports, accounting links and stamp rules remain unchanged.

Company and inventory administration is accessed through the Management navigation group. The duplicate Companies & inventory sidebar footer shortcut is removed.

### Customer and vendor sub-accounts

Customer and vendor Add/Edit forms include Sub-account of and Active/Inactive controls. Lists provide All/Active/Inactive filters and display the parent name; A4/PDF/CSV/Excel list exports include the parent and status. Each sub-account retains its own balance, currency and AR/AP ledger link; parent links do not consolidate balances or change posting. Existing duplicate-name, phone and TRN rules still apply.

Parents must be the same contact type and company; sales users can select only their own customers. Self-links and cycles are rejected. Active children must be moved or made inactive before their parent is deactivated; parents with children cannot be deleted. Inactive contacts remain in history and can be reactivated. New trading documents reject inactive parties, while payment lists retain them for balance settlement and credit/return workflows remain available.

Authorized users can also Activate/Make inactive directly from a contact row. This narrow action preserves identity details and rejects stale status changes.

- **Invoice line Pending Cart:** one **Pending** button (top bar and next to Save record / Save changes, for new and saved invoices) moves every saleable invoice line to the Pending Cart at once (per-line Restore and Restore all before saving); atomic save to non-posting customer drafts in Sales → Pending Cart, with stock/accounting reversal for posted lines and payment, packing, source-link and revision protection.

- **Complete invoice series customization:** administrators can replace the full invoice format per inventory, including fixed text, separators, suffixes, optional company/prefix placeholders, number padding and next sequence. Live previews use the same formatter as posting; saved invoice numbers remain unchanged, with duplicate and stale-editor protection.

### Business dashboard

Sidebar → **Dashboard** (`app/business-dashboard.tsx`) summarises the selected company and inventory for one reporting period. A date filter at the top — **Today**, **This week**, **This month** (default), **Last month**, **This quarter**, **This year** or **Custom range** (From/To) — drives every figure; presets use the same date rules as the Report Center (weeks Monday–Sunday, calendar quarters and years), and **Refresh** reloads. No figure is calculated on the page: each comes from an existing report through `/api/reports`, so it matches that report for the same period and inventory.

- **Trading** (from the Complete Business Final Report): Net sales, Purchases, Net profit (with net margin) and VAT payable / recoverable (output less recoverable input VAT). The first three show the change against the equally long period just before.
- **Position at the period end**: Cash & bank (closing bank balance), Receivables, Payables, and Inventory value (current stock snapshot).
- **Income vs expenses**: posted income and expenses for the six months ending with the period's last month (Income & Expense Graph), with a legend and per-month hover values.
- **Profit & VAT for the period**: income, expenses and cost of sales, net profit, output VAT, recoverable input VAT and the net VAT position.
- **Top customers, Top suppliers, Top-selling items**: the five largest by net sales or net purchases ex VAT (Sales by Customer, Purchases by Supplier and Sales by Item summaries) with their share of the total.
- **Operations & contacts** (live): low-stock and out-of-stock items, open sales and purchase orders, active customers and suppliers.

Every card, line and list opens its source report for the same period (as-of reports use the period end): Sales by Customer, Purchases by Supplier, Profit & Loss, VAT Summary, Bank Register, A/R and A/P Aging, Stock Valuation, Stock Status, Sales Order Fulfilment, Open Purchase Orders, Active Customers and Supplier Contact List. A widget that fails shows its own error while the rest load. Roles without report access (Purchasing, Inventory) see record counts and the daily activity list only. The layout is one column on phones, two on tablets and four across on wide screens; the date presets wrap so all stay visible.

### Insights daily transaction activity

At the bottom of the Dashboard, **Transactions entered on this day** includes an Entry date selector (today in UAE time by default), Today and Refresh actions, a matching entry count and paginated access to every permitted transaction entered that day. Rows show entry time, original document date, type/reference, party, inventory, creator, status and amount in the recorded currency, with View to open the source. Backdated documents appear on their actual entry day. Company, inventory and server-side role restrictions apply. The entry date starts on the period end when that is before today (otherwise today) and can be changed on its own. Sales representatives keep their dedicated My daily work screen and do not have the Dashboard.

## Product search in item pickers

Product dropdowns on sales and invoice lines, bill lines, Stock Transfers and the Inventory invoice editor have a search box. It matches SKU, item number, name, description, model / part number, UPC, item serial number, category and specifications; each option also shows its item number, part number and UPC. Up to 200 matches are listed at a time.

When the same product is on several lines of one form, each line shows the stock left after the other lines: Stock Transfers (option label, Available and the quantity limit, with a check before saving), new sales-side documents such as invoices (Stock in the picker and Available under the item, flagged when a line asks for more), and the Inventory invoice editor (item changes and free lines take stock; stock returns give it back). Saved documents being edited already have their own quantities deducted, so their picker shows current stock.

## Clear company data

Company Setup is laid out like Admin Controls. Four status cards (Company with TRN, Company stamp, Bank accounts with their currencies, Login page) flag missing items with **Action needed** and open their section. A tab bar (Identity, Stamp, Bank accounts, E-invoicing, Layout Builder, Login page, Fonts & text, and Clear company for those allowed) shows one section at a time; the last tab is remembered, and unsaved edits survive switching tabs. Each section says how it is saved: name, logos and stamp with the sticky **Save Company Setup** bar (shown on the Identity and Stamp tabs); bank accounts, e-invoicing and fonts & text with their own buttons; each logo can also be saved alone. Logos and login images sit side by side on wider screens; bank accounts by currency wrap to 2–3 columns until very wide screens; Clear company is styled as a danger zone.

Company Setup → **Fonts & text** (administrators) sets the app font and text sizes for everyone signed in to the company. The font list has the built-in default, fonts installed on most computers (Arial, Tahoma, Segoe UI, Verdana, Georgia, Times New Roman) and self-hosted web fonts (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Poppins, Source Sans 3, Nunito Sans, and the Arabic + Latin fonts Cairo, Tajawal, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Almarai, Readex Pro, Noto Sans Arabic, Noto Kufi Arabic); Latin-only fonts show Arabic text in Noto Sans Arabic. Text size has a slider (80–140%, steps of 5) for each area: **Base text** (everything not listed), **Sidebar**, **Tables and lists**, **Forms and inputs**, **Headings**, and **Printed reports** (report tables printed or saved as PDF from the browser). Each area is independent of the others. A live preview shows a sidebar, heading, field, table and printed-report sample in English and Arabic; **Reset to default**, **Undo changes** and **Save fonts and text** sit below it. The setting applies as CSS variables on the page root, so every screen follows it; sizes written as fixed pixels (for example `text-[11px]`) are not scaled. Printed invoices and other business documents keep the fonts and sizes set in Layout Builder, and jsPDF exports keep their own sizes. Clearing **Company setup and templates** resets the font and sizes to the default.

Company Setup → Clear lets the assigned company Administrator (never All-Admin) clear selected data. The options are grouped, and each shows how many records it would clear (for example Transactions: Sales 120 · Purchases 40 · Banking 30 · Journal entries 200), or "Nothing to clear":
- **Accounting and stock:** Transactions, payments and cheques (with packing lists, VAT returns and their attachments; account and contact balances reset to zero); **Reset stock to zero, keep items**, which also clears warehouse stock, moves between warehouses and stock holds and runs together with Transactions (selected automatically).
- **Customers, vendors and employees** separately: customers with their warranty slips; vendors (warranty slips keep the slip without the supplier link); employees with their attachments.
- **Operations:** Warranty / RMA slips; Today offers and stock holds; Cargo list; RCM declarations.
- **Reports, documents and messages:** Saved reports and attachments (memorised reports, inventory checks, salary sheets, income tax working papers, every remaining attachment); Email templates and remembered email addresses; Notifications.
- **Settings and setup:** VAT, currencies and company controls (standard UAE VAT codes restored); Company setup and templates.

**Select all** and **Clear selection** help choose; a red summary lists what will be cleared with the record total and suggests a backup first. Clearing needs the company name typed exactly, the confirmation tick and the administrator password. The Chart of Accounts and every sub-account, items with their inventory locations and warehouses, the company name and base currency, users and the audit history are always kept.

## Register row actions

In transaction registers (Sales, Purchases and others) the row actions sit on one line in fixed slots (view, attachments, delivery, edit, delete), so each icon lines up down the column; a slot stays empty when that action does not apply to the row. Every saved document has the attachments (paperclip) action: quotations, estimates, proformas, sales orders, invoices, pending invoices, sales receipts, credit memos and customer payments can be changed by users who write sales documents; bills, item receipts, purchase orders, purchase returns, expenses and supplier payments by users who write purchases; cheques, deposits and transfers by users who write banking. Administrators can change any; everyone else with access can view and download.

## Stock holds

Administrators and accountants can put stock on hold for a sales rep from Inventory Overview: **Hold** on a product opens a form for the inventory, quantity (up to the free stock), duration (4 hours, 1 day, 2 days or custom up to 7 days), the sales rep (an active employee) and a note. The same form lists the product's current holds on the chosen inventory (quantity, sales rep, note, time left) with **Unhold** to release one early. Inventory Overview shows a **Stock on hold** panel with time left and **Release**, an "on hold" badge on the product and "on hold · free" under its quantity. While a hold is active, only documents for that sales rep may use the held quantity: invoices, sales receipts and invoice edits by other reps, purchase returns and stock transfers (unless for the same rep) are blocked with a clear message, even with the admin negative-stock override. On a new invoice or sales receipt, choosing a held product fills in only the free quantity, the product list shows "on hold" next to its stock, and the line shows how many are on hold, for which sales rep and for how long; asking for more turns the note red and saving is refused. Holds end automatically when they expire; releases and creations are audited, and holds are included in company backups.

## Inventory Overview filters and price adjustment

Besides search and the top filter bar (All / per-inventory / In stock / Low stock / **On hold**; the Out of stock chip was removed, and rows still show each item's stock status / **Received 7 days** / **30 days**), Inventory Overview filters by **Category**, **Brand**, **Condition** (BRAND NEW, OPEN BOX, REFURBISHED, RENEWED or USED as written in the product's specifications or description; products without one are "Not specified") and **Selling price (excl. VAT)** range, with Clear filters. Brand is a well-known brand found in the item name, then its description or specifications, otherwise the first word of the name. **Price +** adds a percentage or a fixed amount to the prices shown and shared (WhatsApp, Telegram, email and Excel, whose price column notes the increase), on top of which Including VAT still applies; saved selling prices never change.
